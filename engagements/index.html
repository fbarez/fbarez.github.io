Consulting Services

My consulting practice focuses on helping organizations navigate technical AI safety challenges:

  • AI Safety Assessment: Evaluating AI systems for potential risks and alignment issues

  • Interpretability for LLMs: Developing techniques to understand and explain large language model behavior

  • Technical Standards & Policy: Advising on AI governance and responsible development

Academic Services

  • Co-organized the first workshop on Mechanistic Interpretability at ICML 2024

  • Programme chair, reviewer, and workshop organizer at major AI conferences (NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL, ICML)

  • Mentor for PhD, MSc, and BSc students in AI safety research

Media Engagement

As an Oxford University expert, I engage with technical and general media to communicate AI safety concepts to broader audiences, including technical conferences, podcasts, and expert commentary for publications.
