I'm a Research Fellow at [Torr Vision Group (TVG)](https://torrvision.com/index.html), [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), where I lead safety research. Additionally, I'm a Research Advisor at [Apart Research](https://apartresearch.com/). I also hold affiliations with the [Centre for the Study of Existential Risk](https://www.cser.ac.uk/) at University of Cambridge and [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/).

Previously, I worked as Technology and Security Policy Fellow at RAND, on Interpretability at Amazon and The DataLab, safe recommender systems at Huawei, and on building a finance tool for budget management for economic scenario forecasting at Natwest Group.
