I'm a Research Fellow at [Torr vision Group (TVG)](https://torrvision.com/index.html), [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), where I lead the safety research. I'm also a Technology and Security Policy fellow at [RAND](https://www.rand.org/) and the Co-Director and Head of research at [Apart Research](https://apartresearch.com/). I'm also affiliated with the centre for the Study of Existential Risks at University of Cambridge and [Future of Life Institute](https://futureoflife.org/).

Prior to that, I worked on Interpretability at Amazon, safe recommender systems at Huawei and on building a finance tool for budget management for economic scenario forecasting at Natwest Group. I hold a PhD in AI with focus on safety and interpretability and previously studied an MSc in Statistics and BA (Hons) in Economics.
