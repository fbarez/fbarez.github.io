I lead the [Technical Safety & Governance (TSG) Lab](https://tsglab.github.io/) at the University of Oxford, where I am a Senior Researcher and a Governing Body Fellow in AI and Machine Learning at Reuben College. I also serve as Technical Director of the [AI Governance Initiative](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/) and Principal Scientist at [Martian](https://www.withmartian.com/).
I'm interested in intelligence in all its forms. Most of my day-to-day work involves looking inside neural nets to understand how they work, and asking whether that understanding can help us evaluate, control, and govern increasingly capable AI systems. My work is supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, Schmidt Sciences, NVIDIA, and others.

**Affiliations:** [Centre for the Study of Existential Risk](https://www.cser.ac.uk/), University of Cambridge; [Institute for Language, Cognition and Computation](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/ilcc), University of Edinburgh; European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems ([ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/)); and [Digital Trust Centre](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/dtc), Nanyang Technological University.
