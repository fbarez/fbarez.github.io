I am a [Senior Researcher](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/fazl-barez/) at the University of Oxford, where I lead the [Technical Safety & Governance (TSG) Lab](https://tsglab.github.io/) and serve as Technical Director of the [AI Governance Initiative](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/). I am also Principal Scientist at [Martian](https://www.withmartian.com/). My research focuses on understanding what happens inside neural networks and using those insights to make AI systems safer and more governable. My work is supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, Schmidt Sciences, NVIDIA, and others.
