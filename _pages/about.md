I lead the [Technical Safety & Governance (TSG) Lab](https://tsglab.github.io/) at the [University of Oxford](https://eng.ox.ac.uk/people/fazl-barez/) and serve as Technical Director of the [AI Governance Initiative](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/). I am also Principal Scientist at [Martian](https://www.withmartian.com/). My research focuses on understanding what happens inside neural networks and using those insights to make AI systems safer and more governable. My work is supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, Schmidt Sciences, NVIDIA, and others.

**Affiliations:** [Centre for the Study of Existential Risk](https://www.cser.ac.uk/), University of Cambridge; Institute for Language, Cognition and Computation, University of Edinburgh; European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems ([ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/)); and [Digital Trust Centre](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/dtc), Nanyang Technological University.
