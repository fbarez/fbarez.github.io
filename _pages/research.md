## **Research Overview**

My work is dedicated to ensuring that as AI systems become more advanced, they remain safe, reliable, and beneficial. I combine deep technical investigation with cross-disciplinary insights from policy, cognitive science, and governance to address both current and emerging challenges in AI.

### Mechanistic Interpretability

I analyze the inner workings of large language models by deciphering neural circuits, emergent behaviors, and information processing mechanisms. This work not only improves our technical understanding but also informs safety strategies and policy decisions. In recent efforts, I have extended these insights to pinpoint vulnerabilities (e.g., adversarial attacks and jailbreak scenarios) and enhance system robustness.

### Safety and Alignment

I develop techniques to detect and mitigate deceptive behaviors, measure value alignment, and implement robust intervention methods. By rigorously testing model capabilities and limitations, my research drives the creation of specific safety metrics and benchmarks that are crucial for establishing industry standards.

### Technical Evaluation and Standards Development

I lead efforts in systematic testing and benchmarking of AI systems, laying the groundwork for technical evaluations that can be translated into concrete safety standards. This includes establishing protocols that ensure AI technologies meet rigorous performance and reliability criteria.

### Policy Integration and Ethical Deployment

Bridging the gap between technical insights and real-world applications, I work on translating research outcomes into actionable policy frameworks and ethical guidelines. This approach is critical for shaping responsible AI deployment and mitigating long-term societal impacts.

### Fairness and Societal Impact

Beyond technical dimensions, I examine fairness and the ethical implications of AI. My goal is to develop frameworks that promote equitable, inclusive, and responsible AI systems—ensuring that advances in technology benefit all segments of society.
